Learn Atoms

Learn about atoms and their basic structure.

📚 What is an Atom?

An atom is the smallest basic unit of an element that retains the properties of that element. Everything around us is made from atoms.

🔬 Parts of an Atom

An atom contains three main types of particles: protons, neutrons and electrons.

🔴 Proton

Protons have a positive electric charge and are found in the nucleus.

⚪ Neutron

Neutrons have no electric charge and are found in the nucleus.

🔵 Electron

Electrons have a negative electric charge and move around the nucleus.

🎯 The Nucleus

The nucleus is the central part of an atom. It contains protons and neutrons and holds most of the atom's mass.

💡 Simple Example

Example:

A helium atom has 2 protons and normally 2 electrons. It also usually has 2 neutrons.

This shows how different particles make up an atom.

🧠 Quick Summary

Atoms are the basic building blocks of matter. Protons are positive, neutrons have no charge, and electrons are negative. Protons and neutrons are in the nucleus, while electrons occupy the space around it.
