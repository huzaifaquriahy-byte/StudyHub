📚 What are Numbers?

Numbers are used to count, measure and solve problems. Examples include 1, 2, 3, 10, 100 and many more.

➕ Basic Operations

Addition (+), subtraction (-), multiplication (×) and division (÷) are basic mathematical operations.

✏️ Example

5 + 3 = 8
10 - 4 = 6
4 × 2 = 8
12 ÷ 3 = 4
