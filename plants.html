Learn Plants

Discover plant parts and learn how plants grow.

📚 What are Plants?

Plants are living organisms that can make their own food. They need sunlight, water, air and nutrients to grow.

🌱 Main Parts of a Plant

Plants have different parts that perform important jobs.

🌿 Roots

Roots hold the plant in the soil and absorb water and minerals.

🌱 Stem

The stem supports the plant and helps transport water and nutrients.

🍃 Leaves

Leaves help plants make food using sunlight.

☀️ Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process plants use to make food. Plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to produce food and release oxygen.

Sunlight + Water + Carbon Dioxide → Food + Oxygen

💧 What Do Plants Need?

Plants need sunlight, water, carbon dioxide, nutrients and suitable conditions to grow properly.

Example:

A plant kept near sunlight and given enough water can grow healthy when it has suitable soil and nutrients.

🧠 Quick Summary

Plants are living organisms that can make their own food. Roots absorb water, stems support the plant, and leaves help make food through photosynthesis.
