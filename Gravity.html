Learn Gravity

Understand how gravity affects objects around us.

📚 What is Gravity?

Gravity is a force that attracts objects toward each other. On Earth, gravity pulls objects toward the ground.

🌍 Gravity on Earth

Earth's gravity keeps us on the ground and makes objects fall when we drop them.

g ≈ 9.8 m/s²

🍎 Example of Gravity

When you drop an apple, Earth pulls the apple downward. This is an everyday example of gravity.

Example:

If you throw a ball upward, gravity eventually pulls the ball back toward Earth.

🪐 Gravity in Space

Gravity also exists in space. The Sun's gravity keeps planets in orbit, while the Earth's gravity keeps the Moon in orbit around Earth.

🧠 Quick Summary

Gravity is a force of attraction. Earth's gravity pulls objects toward the ground. Gravity also keeps planets and moons in their orbits.
