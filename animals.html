Learn About Animals

Explore animals and learn about their characteristics.

📚 What are Animals?

Animals are living organisms that need food, water and oxygen to survive. Most animals can move from one place to another and respond to their surroundings.

🐾 Animal Groups

Animals can be grouped in different ways. Some common groups include mammals, birds and fish.

🦁

Mammals

Mammals have hair or fur and feed their babies with milk.

🦅

Birds

Birds have feathers, wings and beaks.

🐟

Fish

Fish usually live in water and breathe using gills.

🏠 Animal Habitats

A habitat is the natural home of an animal. Different animals live in different environments such as forests, oceans, deserts and grasslands.

Example:

A camel is well adapted to live in a desert, while many fish are adapted to live in water.

🍎 What Do Animals Need?

Animals need food, water, oxygen and a suitable habitat to survive. Different animals eat different types of food.

🧠 Quick Summary

Animals are living organisms that need food, water and oxygen. They live in different habitats and have different body structures and characteristics.
