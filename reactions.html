📚 What is a Chemical Reaction?

A chemical reaction is a process in which substances change into new substances with different properties.

⚗️ Reactants and Products

The substances that take part in a chemical reaction are called reactants. The new substances formed are called products.

Reactants → Chemical Reaction → Products

🔥 Signs of a Chemical Reaction

Some common signs of a chemical reaction include a change in color, formation of gas, production of heat or light, and formation of a new solid.

🧪 Simple Example

Example:

When vinegar reacts with baking soda, bubbles of gas are produced.

This is evidence that a chemical reaction is taking place.

🔬 Types of Simple Reactions

🔥 Combustion

A substance reacts with oxygen and releases energy.

🔄 Combination

Two or more substances combine to form a new substance.

💥 Decomposition

One substance breaks down into simpler substances.

🧠 Quick Summary

Chemical reactions change reactants into products. Reactions can show signs such as gas, color change, heat, light or a new solid. Chemical reactions are common in everyday life.
