📖 What is Vocabulary?

Vocabulary means the words that a person knows and uses when speaking, reading and writing.

🌟 Useful Words

Happy — feeling good or joyful.
Brave — not afraid of difficult situations.
Beautiful — very pleasing to see.

🏫 Everyday Words

School — a place where students learn.
Friend — a person you like and trust.
Teacher — a person who teaches students.

✏️ Example Sentences

I am happy today.
He is a brave boy.
My teacher is helpful.
